Privacy Policy for Polar

Last updated September 17, 2026 · Applies to the Polar mobile app (Android & iOS)

Polar is a circular chess game built by Kerygma Code. This page explains, in plain terms, what the app collects, why, and the choices you have — covering online multiplayer, ads, the optional Remove Ads purchase, and basic gameplay logging.

Information we collect

Polar is built to need as little about you as possible. Here is everything the app touches, organized by the feature that needs it.

Online multiplayer

When you tap "Play online," the app signs your device in anonymously through Firebase Authentication. This assigns a random ID to your device — it is not tied to your name, email address, or any account, and Polar never asks you to create one.

Stored

Your anonymous device ID, plus the moves and room codes needed to keep a live match in sync between two devices. This is stored only as long as it's needed to run your game — not linked to you personally in any way.

Advertising

The free version of Polar shows an ad (via Google AdMob) after each finished game. To select and measure these ads, Google's AdMob SDK may collect device and advertising identifiers on its own behalf. Polar does not collect or see this data itself — it flows directly to Google under Google's own privacy policy, linked below.

Purchases

The optional $1.99 "Remove Ads" purchase is handled entirely by Google Play Billing. Polar never receives, sees, or stores your payment details — card numbers, billing address, and so on stay with Google. The app only keeps a small flag on your own device recording that you've purchased Remove Ads, so it knows not to show ads anymore.

Gameplay logging

To help with balancing and testing, Polar sends a small, anonymous record of each finished game — things like how it ended, how many moves it took, how long it lasted, and whether you played an AI or another person. This never includes your name, moves you made, or any way to identify you personally.

How we use it

  • To run online multiplayer — relaying moves between the two devices in a match.
  • To show and measure ads — via Google AdMob, for players on the free tier.
  • To remember your purchase — so Remove Ads stays off once you've bought it.
  • To improve the game — aggregate stats on how games actually play out (length, outcomes, difficulty balance) shape future updates.

We do not use anything Polar collects for advertising profiles of our own, and we do not sell any information to anyone.

Third-party services

Polar is built on a small number of well-known services, all operated by Google:

Firebase (Authentication & Firestore)

Anonymous sign-in and match syncing for online multiplayer.

Google AdMob

Serves the interstitial ad shown between games on the free tier.

Google Play Billing

Processes the one-time Remove Ads purchase.

Google Apps Script

Receives the anonymous, per-game log described above.

Each is covered by Google's Privacy Policy, and AdMob specifically by Google's ads policy.

Data sharing

We don't sell your data, and we don't share it with anyone beyond the service providers above, each of which processes it only to provide the specific feature it powers (multiplayer sync, ads, or purchases).

Children's privacy

Polar does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us using the details below and we'll remove it.

Your choices

  • Ad personalization — you can opt out of personalized ads and reset your advertising identifier from your device's own Settings app (under Google → Ads on Android, or Privacy & Security → Tracking on iOS).
  • Uninstalling — removes the local purchase flag from your device. Any online match you were part of is unaffected on the other player's device until it naturally finishes.
  • Questions or deletion requests — reach out any time using the contact details below.

Data retention

Match data and anonymous IDs are kept only as long as needed to run the features above. Because none of it is tied to your name or any account, there's no personal profile of you for us to retain in the first place.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Continuing to use Polar after a change means you accept the updated policy.
